About us

The Sum & Ledger experience

A tax and bookkeeping firm focused on individuals, real estate, information technology and small businesses. We're currently accepting new clients.

Our firm

Sum & Ledger provides tax preparation and accounting services to individuals and to small and medium-size businesses. We prepare more than 1,500 returns every tax season for clients in all 50 states and U.S. territories, and we support our bookkeeping clients every month of the year.

Our staff is here to support your business and keep your books organized. We do traditional bookkeeping as well as QuickBooks Online and QuickBooks Desktop.

We serve clients in English and Spanish.
